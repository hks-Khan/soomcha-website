Your active calories run the simulation.

Soomcha is an activity-calorie simulation. The active energy you burn — tracked by Apple Health — becomes growth energy for the residents of your town. You choose where they train; every evening the results come in.

01 / How it works

You move.
They train.

One day in Soomcha, from your first step to the evening results.

01Move

Burn active calories

Walk, run, swim, lift — Apple Health counts the active energy you burn.

Apple Health
02Convert

Energy becomes XP

Every 10 kcal gives each resident 1 discipline XP and 1 body-stat XP. It isn’t split between them.

10 kcal = 1 XP
03Train

Pick today’s place

Choose a place the night before. At 09:00 everyone heads out and trains in their own way.

09:00 – 21:00
04Results

See who got stronger

At 21:00 growth settles once. Check the results, then pick tomorrow’s place.
